# Theory

`main.tex` is the canonical theory draft for safe dynamic spectral reduction.

The proof order is deliberate:

1. exact prediction-space gradient energy;
2. exact fixed-geometry finite-horizon deletion value;
3. compute-aware mode oracle;
4. conditioning and finite-sample consequences;
5. moving-eigenspace target transport;
6. curvature-to-spectral-path control;
7. nonlinear structural-flow stability;
8. discarded-cluster persistence through eigengap/operator motion;
9. structural realization and persistence of a fixed mask;
10. exact final-hidden neuron-basis contraction;
11. phase-aware onset in the solvable quadratic teacher--student model;
12. exact post-contraction target-angle dynamics and the premature-contraction discovery delay;
13. fresh-probe safety for repeated adaptive contraction decisions;
14. algorithm only after the above pieces are explicit.

Statements that depend on architecture-specific or empirical assumptions are labeled as such. The draft intentionally avoids the claims that small eigenvalues are automatically bad, that a weight spectrum is a prediction spectrum, or that pruning automatically accelerates all optimization modes.
