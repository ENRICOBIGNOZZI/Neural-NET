# Theory

`main.tex` is the canonical theory draft for safe dynamic spectral reduction.

The proof order is deliberate:

1. exact prediction-space gradient energy;
2. exact fixed-geometry finite-horizon deletion value;
3. compute-aware mode oracle;
4. conditioning and finite-sample consequences;
5. moving-eigenspace target transport;
6. nonlinear projected-flow stability;
7. spectral persistence through eigengap/operator motion;
8. structural realization gap;
9. exact final-hidden neuron-basis contraction;
10. phase-aware onset in the solvable quadratic teacher--student model;
11. algorithm only after the above pieces are explicit.

Statements that depend on architecture-specific or empirical assumptions are labeled as such. The draft intentionally avoids the claim that small eigenvalues are automatically bad or that pruning automatically accelerates all optimization modes.
